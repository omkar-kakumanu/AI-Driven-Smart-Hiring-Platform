# AI / ML Architecture & Safety Guidelines

## Matching Architecture
The matching engine evaluates candidates across 8 weighted criteria:
- **Required Skills Match**: 30%
- **Experience Level Fit**: 20%
- **Education Requirements**: 10%
- **Preferred Skills Match**: 10%
- **Projects Fit**: 10%
- **Certifications**: 5%
- **Semantic Resume-Job Similarity**: 10%
- **Soft Skills**: 5%

## AI Safety & Non-Discrimination
1. **Decision Support**: AI recommendations are explicitly formatted as decision-support insights for human recruiters.
2. **Protected Characteristics Filter**: Protected attributes (race, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, age, ethnicity, nationality) are strictly excluded from all embedding models, ranking algorithms, and interview evaluations.
3. **Explainability**: Every candidate score includes explicit matched skills and missing requirement breakdowns.
